Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

My Kharcha ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal expense tracking application designed to help users manage and understand their everyday financial transactions.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features used by a user, My Kharcha may process transaction-related information such as transaction amount, date, merchant name, transaction type, category, and account or card information such as a masked last four digits when available.

2. SMS and Notifications

If the user grants the required Android permissions, My Kharcha may read relevant transaction messages or notifications to identify expenses and income. This information is used to provide transaction tracking features.

The app is designed to avoid collecting unrelated messages, passwords, OTPs, PINs, CVVs, or other authentication secrets.

3. Email and Gmail

If the user chooses to connect supported Gmail features, My Kharcha may access relevant transaction emails to identify financial transactions and organize them within the app.

Gmail access is used only for the functionality provided by the app and is not used to sell personal information or for advertising.

4. Google Drive Backup

My Kharcha may allow users to back up and restore supported structured app data using their own Google Drive account.

The app uses the Google Drive file access required for its backup functionality. Backup data belongs to the user and is stored in the user's Google Drive.

5. Data Sharing

My Kharcha does not sell users' personal information. Information is not shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

6. Data Security

My Kharcha is designed to limit access to information to what is necessary for the application's features. Users should also protect access to their Android device and Google account.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Users can manage and delete their app data through the application and can delete their Google Drive backup from their own Google Drive.

8. Children's Privacy

My Kharcha is not specifically directed at children under the age required by applicable law, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application or its data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions about My Kharcha, please contact the developer through the contact information associated with the application.
