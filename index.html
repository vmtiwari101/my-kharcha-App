My Kharcha

My Kharcha is a personal expense tracking application designed to help users organize, track, and understand their everyday expenses.

What My Kharcha Does

  • Track personal expenses and income.
  • Organize transactions by categories and subcategories.
  • View transaction history and spending summaries.
  • Help users understand their spending patterns.
  • Support automatic transaction detection from supported sources.
  • Provide Google Drive backup and restore for supported app data.

Privacy

My Kharcha is designed with user privacy in mind. The app only uses permissions and data required for its features.

For details about how information is handled, please read our Privacy Policy.

About My Kharcha

My Kharcha is intended as a general-purpose expense management application that can be used by anyone.
